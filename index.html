Terms of use

Alerts are informational. They carry no recommendation, they can be wrong or false, and nothing here is individual investment advice — you use the service at your own risk.

The chart

A dot marks every alert the service sent on that stock, so you can see where it spoke. One candle is 2 hours. The chart is there for context only — this is an alerts product, not a trading terminal.

What we send

One short message when something meaningful happens: a breakout, a turn, a test of a level that has held for months, a sharp move backed by unusual volume. Each one says what happened, which price level matters, and what it means for a trader and for a long-term holder.

How much you get

Every alert is roughly one a day per stock — everything worth telling you about. Major turns only is a few a month per stock: just the big ones. You can switch at any time, here or in the bot, and pause everything without losing your list.
